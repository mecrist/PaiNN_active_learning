METHODOLOGICAL NOTES & CAVEATS:
1. In-Distribution vs Out-of-Distribution Energy Accuracy (*):
   - PaiNN was trained after subtracting isolated per-element atomic reference energies (E0_Si, E0_O, E0_H).
   - On these 70 held-out test frames (randomly sampled from the same bulk and slab AIMD trajectories), the residual
     energy is narrow (~0.012 eV/atom), which PaiNN fits to 2.71 meV/atom RMSE.
   - When applied out-of-distribution to the 5 Å water-silica slab interface in sep_pax, PaiNN began with an Energy RMSE
     of 276.88 meV/atom, which active learning and post-AL convergence successfully reduced to 74.26 meV/atom (-73.2%).
   - In contrast, MACE directly predicts total potential energy without empirical pre-subtraction, generalizing
     robustly to sep_pax with an initial Energy RMSE of 31.20 meV/atom and converging post-AL to 30.12 meV/atom.
2. Force Parity:
   - Both MACE (~316 meV/Å) and PaiNN (~318 meV/Å) achieved nearly identical force accuracy on the pre-AL test set.
